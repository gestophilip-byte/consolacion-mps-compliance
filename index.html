Hourly reporting directive

All patrollers are directed to submit a Situation Report (SITREP) every one (1) hour through the designated communication channel. Even when there is no untoward incident, the patroller must report: “Situation normal; no untoward incident reported.” Failure to submit without valid justification shall be recorded and dealt with accordingly.
